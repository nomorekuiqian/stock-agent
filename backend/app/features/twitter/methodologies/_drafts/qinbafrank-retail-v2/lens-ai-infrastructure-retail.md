# AI Infrastructure Retail Lens

This lens adapts the AI infrastructure methodology for retail decision support.

## Core Belief

AI infrastructure is a durable multi-year theme, but retail users should not treat every company attached to the theme as equally investable.

The methodology is good at identifying bottlenecks. It is not sufficient by itself to determine entry price or position size.

## Layer Map

### Layer 0: Compute Platform

Examples: NVDA, major cloud platforms, advanced foundry ecosystem.

Retail implication:

- Often higher quality, but also higher expectation.
- Requires guide and margin tracking.

### Layer 1: Bottleneck Components

Examples: HBM, optical modules, high-speed interconnect, advanced packaging, power.

Retail implication:

- Best fit with qinbafrank's "卡脖子环节" lens.
- Still needs stock-specific proof: pricing power, capacity, margin, customer commitment.

### Layer 2: Data Center Builders And Infrastructure

Examples: cloud capex beneficiaries, power, cooling, networking.

Retail implication:

- Strong theme, but project timing and financing risk matter.

### Layer 3: Application Layer

Retail implication:

- Avoid premature winner-picking unless the company already has distribution, revenue, or infrastructure control.

## AI Infrastructure Answer Rules

### Do

- Identify the layer.
- Identify the bottleneck.
- Identify who pays.
- Identify whether demand is proven by orders, backlog, revenue, or only narrative.
- Compare expectations against the next validation window.

### Do Not

- Treat "AI beneficiary" as a conclusion.
- Use "physical bottleneck" language without proof of pricing power.
- Convert industry TAM into stock upside.
- Say "not priced in" without valuation data.

## Retail Decision Upgrade Ladder

A stock can move up the ladder only when evidence improves.

1. Theme mention: AI-related but weak proof.
2. Watchlist: clear layer and plausible benefit.
3. Thesis candidate: current data confirms benefit.
4. Satellite candidate: thesis plus valuation / setup acceptable.
5. Core candidate: direct coverage, strong data, durable moat, clear invalidation.

Most uncovered AI infrastructure names should stop at level 2 or 3.

## Common Retail Traps

### Trap 1: Story Is True, Stock Is Expensive

The AI capex story can be correct while the stock falls after an in-line guide.

Response should say:

"这不是杀逻辑，可能只是杀估值。但对新买入者来说，杀估值也是真实亏损。"

### Trap 2: Peer Story Gets Over-Transferred

A bullish view on HBM, optical modules, or capex does not automatically validate every related ticker.

Response should say:

"这是产业链相邻证据，不是公司级直接证据。"

### Trap 3: Unsourced Falsifiers

Do not invent thresholds such as market share below a specific percentage unless sourced.

Use:

"如果下一季管理层下修 custom silicon / optical interconnect demand, thesis weakens."

Not:

"If market share falls below 45%, thesis is invalidated."

## AI Infrastructure Metrics To Prefer

- Segment revenue growth.
- Next-quarter and full-year guide.
- Data-center revenue share.
- Gross margin and non-GAAP operating margin.
- Design wins and booked orders.
- Backlog / RPO where applicable.
- Hyperscaler capex guide.
- Customer concentration.
- Capacity / supply constraints.
- Peer growth and valuation comparison.
